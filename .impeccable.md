## Design Context

### Users
Brand-side marketers and producers at gaming publishers/studios evaluating an influencer marketing partner; secondary audiences include creators/influencers and talent managers researching the agency. Primary jobs: understand capability, trust the team, browse case studies, and make contact.

### Brand Personality
Confident, editorial, professional — gaming-industry credibility without "gamer aesthetic" clichés. Voice is direct and premium; the site should feel like a sharp agency book, not a SaaS landing page.

### Aesthetic Direction
Premium editorial gaming-influencer agency for 2026: Syne (display) + Satoshi (body) + Roboto Mono (UI meta), deep ink `#161616` on richer warm paper `#faf7ef`, with soft ink-tint hairlines, ink bands used sparingly for chapter breaks, and a restrained olive accent `#3f4a35`. Type carries the brand — oversized Syne moments, clear hierarchy, calm Satoshi measure. Spatial composition is asymmetric and intentional (scale jumps, breathing room), not timid equal-column stacks. Portfolio grids and case studies feel curated and tactile. Light mode only.

**Mandate (Max, 2026):** complete visual redesign toward a minimum 9/10 agency site — not Webflow fidelity. Prefer confident editorial drama over migration parity when they conflict.

**Anti-references:** generic AI/SaaS (cyan-on-dark, purple gradients, glassmorphism, Inter, hero-metric card grids), loud esports neon, cookie-cutter startup tropes (logo cloud + feature grid + big CTA spam), and timid hairline-divider-everywhere layouts.

### Design Principles
1. **Brand-true boldness** — stay in the ink-on-paper system, but prefer confident editorial drama over timid Webflow parity.
2. **Editorial hierarchy** — type and purposeful section architecture carry the brand; decoration is sparse and intentional.
3. **Case studies convert** — portfolio clarity and mobile readability matter as much as desktop polish.
4. **Contact must work** — the CTA path (form + response) is a production gate, not a nice-to-have.
5. **Responsive as adaptation** — every breakpoint should feel designed, not crushed.
